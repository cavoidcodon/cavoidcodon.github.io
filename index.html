Automotive software

Notes and training material

A collection of interesting topics in automotive software, from software platforms and architecture to communication and diagnostics. I write these notes to share what I have learned along the way, and I hope they serve as a practical reference for anyone who is interested in the field.

SOME/IP and SOME/IP Service Discovery

A guide to SOME/IP, the service-oriented middleware protocol used in many vehicle networks, and to its Service Discovery protocol (SOME/IP-SD): the message format, serialization, transport over UDP and TCP, and how services are offered, found and subscribed at run time.

UDS (ISO 14229)

A guide to Unified Diagnostic Services, the protocol a tester uses to talk to an ECU: the request and response format and negative response codes, diagnostic sessions and security access, reading and writing data by identifier, routine control, reading and clearing DTCs, the ECU reprogramming sequence, and how UDS runs over CAN (ISO-TP) and Ethernet (DoIP).

QNX

QNX Neutrino RTOS

A guide to QNX, the real-time operating system behind many digital cockpits and ADAS controllers: its microkernel architecture, synchronous message passing, resource managers, processes, threads and priority scheduling, adaptive partitioning, and how it hosts platforms such as AUTOSAR Adaptive.
